Bitcoin Tools

Enter a 20-byte hash160 (40 hex characters) to get all possible address formats.

40 characters 0-9, a-f. Click a coin name to collapse or expand its section; your choice is remembered.

Enter a Bitcoin or TRON address to get its 20-byte hex id (hash160 / account id).

Supports Bitcoin, Litecoin (L/M), Dogecoin (D), Dash (X), TRON (T), and Bech32. Only 20-byte payloads are supported.

Enter a private key (hex or WIF) to derive the Bitcoin address. Uses compressed public key.

Hex: 64 characters 0-9, a-f. WIF: Base58Check (starts with 5, L, K for mainnet or 9, c for testnet).

Enter a public key (hex) to get all possible address formats. Compressed (66 hex) or uncompressed (130 hex).

Compressed: 66 chars (02/03 + 32-byte x). Uncompressed: 130 chars (04 + x + y).
